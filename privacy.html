Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains how the Chat Lost It Uploader app ("the App") handles data.

1. Data we access

When the account owner connects TikTok or YouTube, the App receives an access token and basic account info (such as the account's open ID and display name) so it can publish videos to that account and read that account's own video statistics (views, likes, comments, watch time).

2. How we use it

Only to upload and publish the owner's videos and to show the owner how their videos perform. Nothing else.

3. Storage

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. We do not run servers that store this data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Data is only sent to TikTok or YouTube as needed to post videos.

5. Other people's data

The App does not collect data about viewers or any other users.

6. Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or in your Google account permissions, and the stored tokens can be deleted from the owner's computer. Email us to request deletion of anything else.
